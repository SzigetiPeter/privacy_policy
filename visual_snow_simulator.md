# Privacy Policy - Visual Snow Data Project

**Effective Date:** 2026-02-25

The Visual Snow Data Project ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and user responsibility for the Visual Snow Android application and associated website.

## 1. Zero-Knowledge Architecture
The Visual Snow application is designed with a **Zero-Knowledge** architecture. This means:
*   **No Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data, symptom logs, or configurations on any external servers.
*   **No Network Access:** The application does not request internet permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET`). It is technically incapable of sending data off your device.
*   **Local Storage only:** All data you input (symptom logs, visual settings, notes) is stored strictly within your the device's private sandbox storage.

## 2. User Responsibility & Data Ownership
You are the sole owner and guardian of your data.
*   **Data Portability:** You may choose to export your data (e.g., as PDF reports or ZIP backups). Once exported, the security and privacy of this data are your sole responsibility.
*   **Data Loss:** Because we do not have access to your data, we cannot recover it if you delete the app, lose your device, or if the device fails. You are responsible for managing your own backups via the export tools provided.
*   **Third-Party Sharing:** If you choose to share your reports with medical professionals or researchers, you do so at your own risk.

## 3. No Responsibility for User Actions
The creator of the Visual Snow Data Project is **not responsible** for:
*   How you interpret the data logged in the app.
*   Any medical decisions made based on the app's output (see Medical Disclaimer).
*   The consequences of you sharing your data with third parties.
*   Unauthorized access to your data on your device.

## 4. Permissions
The app requests the following permissions for functional purposes only:
*   **Camera:** Used to provide a live background for the visual snow simulation overlays. The app does not save or transmit images or video from the camera.
