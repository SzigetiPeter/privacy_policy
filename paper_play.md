# Privacy Policy for PaperPlay

**Last updated:** December 06, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how **PaperPlay** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "Service").

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**PaperPlay** is designed to function primarily as an offline, local application. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on external servers. All data generated or processed by the application remains on your device.

### 1.1 Audio Recordings
The app uses your device's microphone to record audio.
-   **Data Usage:** Audio recordings are stored locally on your device's internal storage or database.
-   **Sharing:** These recordings are not shared with us or any third parties unless you explicitly choose to share the file using your device's native sharing capabilities.

### 1.2 NFC Data
The app reads and writes data to NFC tags.
-   **Data Usage:** This data is processed locally to link audio files to specific NFC tags.
-   **Sharing:** No NFC data is transmitted to external servers.

### 1.3 Device Permissions
To provide its features, the app requires the following permissions:
-   **Record Audio:** Required to record voice memos and audio tracks.
-   **Storage (Read/Write):** Required to save recordings and access existing audio/image files on your device to link them to NFC tags.
-   **NFC:** Required to scan and write to NFC tags.
-   **Internet:** While the app requests internet permission, it is primarily used for internal library dependencies (e.g., loading images via Coil). The app does not send analytics or user data to cloud servers.

## 2. Data Retention
Since we do not collect user data on our servers, we do not have a data retention policy for your personal information. You have full control over the data stored on your device and can delete audio recordings or app data at any time via the app or your device settings.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The app may use third-party libraries for functionality (e.g., media playback, image loading). These libraries may collect standard usage data (like crash reports) if configured, but we have not implemented any custom analytics or tracking solutions in this version.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
